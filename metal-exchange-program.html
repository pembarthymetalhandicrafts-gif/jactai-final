What items are accepted

Brass items

Lamps, bowls, puja items, and decor made of brass.

Copper items

Copper bottles, plates, serving pieces, and home accents.

Old utensils

Used or unused kitchen and serving utensils in exchangeable condition.

Damaged decor

Bent, worn, or damaged decorative items that can be reworked.

How valuation works

Share item photos on WhatsApp with approximate weight and dimensions. Our team checks metal type, purity estimate, craftsmanship potential, and condition before confirming your exchange value.

  • Transparent price estimate shared before confirmation
  • No hidden charges during exchange finalization
  • Final value adjusted only after physical inspection

Exchange options

Discount on new products

Use valuation credits to reduce the final price of your selected handcrafted item.

Direct exchange

Swap your approved old item for selected crafts based on equivalent value.

Craft transformation

Your exchanged metal supports artisan-led remaking into culturally inspired craft pieces.

FAQ

Do you accept mixed metal items?

Yes, but value depends on the actual brass/copper content after inspection.

How long does valuation take?

Initial WhatsApp valuation is usually shared within 24 working hours.

Can I exchange from another city?

Yes. We coordinate pickup or shipping guidance based on your location.
